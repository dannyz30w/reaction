About Reactions

REACTIONS is a school publication written by students of the Physical Science Department of Bronx High School of Science. A small group of students from all grade levels craft physics and chemistry related articles, graphics, puzzles, etc., for publication.

The club sees to all aspects of the production of the journal, including ad sales, printing, binding, and journal sales. This occurs under the guidance of an experienced department advisor on an annual basis.

We meet bi-weekly every Wednesday at Room 225 during SGI.

REALM Merit Award Winner

The 2020-21 edition won the REALM Merit Award, an award by the NCTE that recognizes outstandingly well-designed and written journals.
